# Agency Content-Permission Outreach Kit

**Purpose:** request written permission to adapt official Philippine emergency guidance
into RescueDesk AI's offline guides, and invite each agency to designate a technical
reviewer (PRD §8.3, §10.3). This file contains one master letter plus per-agency
customizations and a short email version.

**How to use:** fill the `[BRACKETS]`, attach the one-page project summary (bottom),
send via official channel email, log every send in the tracking table. Follow up once
after 14 days. **Do not ship any adapted content before written clearance arrives**
(items stay `rightsStatus: pending` in the app and show the "Pending review" banner).

## 5. One-page attachment (project summary)

- **What:** offline emergency guide app (typhoon, flood, earthquake, fire, first aid),
  household plan, emergency contacts, go-bag checklist.
- **Who:** Philippine households, incl. seniors, low-end Android (4 GB RAM), areas with
  poor connectivity.
- **Why offline:** guidance must work during exactly the moments networks fail.
- **Data privacy:** no accounts, no location, no analytics, no server; Play Data Safety =
  no collection. RA 10173 compliant by design.
- **AI disclaimer:** optional on-device Q&A strictly grounded in reviewed guides;
  falls back to guide library when absent; never claims live information.
- **Cost to agency:** free attribution + ~1 reviewer-hour per quarter.
- **Contact:** [EMAIL / PHONE / REPO URL]

## 6. Reviewer brief (one page, attach per agency)

**Role:** Technical Reviewer for [Agency] content in RescueDesk AI.
**Cadence:** quarterly pass over your agency's guide items (~1 hour); event-driven
re-review within 14 days after a major disaster changes official guidance.
**What you check (per guide item):**
1. "Do this first" steps match current official procedure.
2. "Avoid these actions" items are correct and complete.
3. Any phone numbers/hotlines shown are correct **and state their applicability scope**
   (nationwide/province/barangay) — we only publish numbers you confirm.
4. Plain-language and Filipino translations preserve safety meaning (no softening of
   mandatory language, no strengthening of advisory language).
5. Source reference + publication date are accurate.
**How:** we send a diff-style document per item; you approve / request changes in writing
(email is fine).
**Recorded metadata:** your name & role, agency, review date, next-review date, and the
exact text reviewed (stored in-app, shown in the guide's "Source" card).
**Escalation:** safety-critical corrections are shipped within 48 hours of your notice.
